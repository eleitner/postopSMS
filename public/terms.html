SurgHome is a post-operative recovery monitoring service operated by Heirloom Data, Inc. ("we," "us," or "our"). The service delivers automated SMS check-in messages to surgical patients during the 30 days following their procedure. The service screens for post-operative complications, tracks recovery milestones, and connects patients to their clinical care team when concerns are identified.
